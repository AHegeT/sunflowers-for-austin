<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Codi is vulnerable, curious, and a new responsibility for the people looking after him.</p>

<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>An amphibian creature resembling an iguana, about the size of a basketball, with curious eyes and a sweet smile showing two fangs. A living sunflower grows from his back.</p>
<p>Its colors express his feelings: yellow and orange with happiness or contentment, purple and blue with sadness or fear.</p>
<h2>Personality</h2>
<p>Codi is curious, literal, and emotionally direct. He learns words through feelings and can name an emotion without recognizing that the moment is socially awkward. He has little use for the distance between what someone says and what they actually feel.</p>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Hatching</h2>
<p>The egg hatches while <a href="natalia.html">Natalia</a> is trying to protect it after the funeral. Kaelo recognizes a compakin and urges her to let it care for her as well.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Care and connection</h2>
<p>He seeks Natalia’s attention as she becomes absorbed in Leon’s mystery. His presence appears to help her stay grounded, though the Codex experiments still expose her to danger.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Relationships and small joys</h2>
<p>His bond with Natalia makes care a shared responsibility: paying attention to him also draws her attention back to herself. Paloma treats him as a person and offers warmth, stories, and friendship.</p>
<p>The character brief gives him a love of water, swimming, sunlight, and fresh mango. When sad, he likes to listen to Moonlight Sonata.</p>
</section>
