<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Natalia is used to analyzing problems, planning her time, and measuring how well she is doing. She approaches an unfamiliar world with the tools and expectations of her professional life in Austin.</p>
<h2>Personality</h2>
<p>She is observant, pragmatic, and demanding of herself. Her analytical confidence sits alongside exhaustion and a protective instinct she does not always know how to express.</p>
