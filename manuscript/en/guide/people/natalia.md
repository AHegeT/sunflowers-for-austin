<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Natalia is used to analyzing problems, planning her time, and measuring how well she is doing. She approaches an unfamiliar world with the tools and expectations of her professional life in Austin.</p>
<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>Light brown skin, dark brown hair usually pulled into a tight bun, and perceptive brown eyes. Her structured black and charcoal clothes come from her professional life in Austin; their wear and her closed posture reflect her exhaustion.</p>
<h2>Personality and skills</h2>
<p>Natalia uses dry sarcasm and analysis to keep painful feelings at a distance. She is competitive, reserved, and demanding of herself, but also capable of fierce loyalty and protection.</p>
<p>She notices posture, pupils, hierarchy, and defensive behavior. At her best, she sees another person’s perspective and builds alliances. Her weakness is over-analysis: she can search for an elaborate psychological explanation while missing hunger, fatigue, or a simple practical need.</p>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Background</h2>
<p>She works with patients experiencing Temporal Dislocation Syndrome and experiences it herself. Leon’s satchel and summons bring her south, where Monty tells her that her former mentor is dead.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Work and abilities</h2>
<p>Natalia becomes a replacement teacher at Marymount. Her memory techniques and somatic override help her deliver lessons, but she initially mistakes the students’ applause for approval.</p><p>Her conversation with Paloma begins to change how she approaches the classroom.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Relationships</h2>
<p><a href="paloma.html">Paloma</a> becomes a partner in investigating Leon’s work. Their different ways of asking questions help them discover the egg.</p><p><a href="monty.html">Monty</a> is both a familiar presence and someone whose experience of the missing years differs sharply from hers.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>At the funeral</h2>
<p><a href="arcturus.html">Arcturus</a> pressures her to return to Austin. While trying to protect the egg, she falls; <a href="kaelo.html">Kaelo</a> helps her as it hatches. She welcomes the newborn <a href="codi.html">Codi</a>.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>In the mountains</h2>
<p>Her attempts to use the Codex and her TDS episodes to investigate the Continuum become increasingly risky. After her collapse, Monty challenges her priorities and tells her information about Paloma’s family that Paloma herself does not know.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Earlier life</h2>
<p>Her mother was Indian and her father American. She grew up in Austin’s corporate world and experienced life in an Oculus Cradle before becoming involved with the Unseen. Kaelo’s teaching was an early inspiration.</p>
<p>She entered the Yosemite Continuum and returned with missing memories. Her chronological age and lived experience do not line up neatly; the precise dates of her uploads remain unsettled.</p>
</section>
