<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>Kenji has an optic implant in his left eye.</p>
<h2>Personality and purpose</h2>
<p>Stoic and pessimistic about the world’s chances, he documents what happened so it cannot be forgotten if society collapses again. He brings up uncomfortable subjects even when others would rather avoid them.</p>
<h2>Relationships</h2>
<p>He distrusts Arcturus while continuing to watch and work with him. He has allied himself with Julia despite condemning her former behavior, and considers Kaelo too optimistic.</p>
<h2>At Leon’s funeral</h2>
<p>He questions how quickly the others reached Leon after his death. His skepticism interrupts the more convenient accounts of what happened.</p>
