<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Arcturus’s status and influence shape how other people interact with him.</p>

<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>A carefully engineered image of strength: a buzzcut, a pronounced jawline, surgically enhanced golden eyes, and suits with shimmering solar-flare patterns. His appearance is part of his pursuit of an ideal self.</p>
<h2>Personality and ambitions</h2>
<p>Controlling, exacting, and sensitive to failure, Arcturus believes his direction is the best way forward. His obsession with perfection coexists with idealism, melancholy, and a private love of poetry and stories.</p>
<p>He wants to save Austin and restore the technological promise he associates with the 2030s. That urgency makes it difficult for him to share responsibility or tolerate opposition.</p>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>At Leon’s funeral</h2>
<p>He pressures <a href="natalia.html">Natalia</a> to return to her work in Austin. <a href="paloma.html">Paloma</a> challenges him openly. Natalia and Paloma also witness his difficult relationship with <a href="julia.html">Julia</a>.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Background and relationships</h2>
<p>He worked at a company involved in creating Oculus, later joined the Unseen, and helped build the Yosemite project. His former collaboration with Leon now carries resentment over their divided work.</p>
<p>Julia is his mother. Their relationship is strained: her attempts to guide him can feel to him like a judgment that he is fundamentally flawed. He tries to define himself apart from her influence.</p>
