<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>He enters the main story at a moment when Natalia needs help protecting the egg.</p>

<h2>Appearance and perception</h2>
<p>Slender, blind, and deaf, Kaelo uses haptic sensors to perceive the world and walks with a cane. He accepts aspects of aging while continuing to test what technology can make possible.</p>
<h2>Personality</h2>
<p>Resilient, hopeful, and intensely curious. He prefers to support causes and people he believes in rather than take direct control of every situation.</p>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>The hatching</h2>
<p>He helps Natalia after her fall, recognizes the newborn compakin, and suggests these creatures may be a way forward.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Earlier life</h2>
<p>His parents were Fatima Al-Shabani, an influential figure in Lamu, and Adebayo Okoro, an ethnomusicologist studying Taarab music. Storms and displacement brought family losses and the injuries behind his sensory changes.</p>
<p>He studied software engineering in Lagos and later worked in the Netherlands. His research into haptic perception contributed to virtual-reality systems used in the Cradles. He secretly worked against Oculus and became an early organizer of the Unseen.</p>
<h2>Connections</h2>
<p>Leon was a close collaborator. Kaelo’s lecture inspired Natalia when she was young. He distrusts Julia’s past but sees the possibility of cooperation, and his restoration work has helped make Nigeria an important revivalist hub.</p>
</section>
