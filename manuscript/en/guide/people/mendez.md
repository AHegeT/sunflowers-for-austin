<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>She controls teaching assignments and the school’s expectations for its new staff.</p>

<h2>Personality and everyday life</h2>
<p>Strict and often disconnected from the students, Mendez fears the chaos that followed the upheavals. She finds reassurance in structure and order.</p>
<p>She loves sweet corn cake and keeps a dog named Matilda in her office, named after her favorite childhood movie.</p>
