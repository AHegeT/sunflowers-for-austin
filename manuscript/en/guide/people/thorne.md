<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>He walks hunched and retains a port in the back of his neck that once connected directly to Oculus’s system.</p>
<h2>Personality</h2>
<p>Suspicious and impatient, Thorne feels the absence of the system he once served. He dislikes bodily modifications and believes there is little time left to repair the world.</p>
<h2>Relationships</h2>
<p>His devotion to Kaelo reflects his belief that Kaelo helped free him. He deeply distrusts Eva Rostova.</p>
<h2>At Leon’s funeral</h2>
<p>He calls Leon a traitor and blames him for the loss of access to the Continuum. He openly rejects Julia’s invitation to work with the Green Hands.</p>
