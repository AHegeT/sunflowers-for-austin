<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Monty offers practical help and a familiar presence as Natalia arrives in a place she barely knows.</p>

<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>Big and physically strong, with calm green eyes, a sun-kissed face, and hands marked by practical work. He is Mexican-Venezuelan and wears functional, repaired clothing.</p>
<h2>Personality and skills</h2>
<p>Patient, sincere, and committed to Cuernavaca, Monty embodies the Green Gloves’ belief that healing takes work. He is slow to anger and protective of the people and places he cares about.</p>
<p>His water engineering and restoration work give him a practical understanding of rebuilding. He hopes to restore lost species, including elephants.</p>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Natalia’s arrival</h2>
<p>He meets <a href="natalia.html">Natalia</a> at the station, tells her Leon is dead, and arranges her stay at Miss Lina’s house.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Leon and the school</h2>
<p>Monty takes over Leon’s botanics classes. He shows Natalia how living-material structures in the lab conceal the room where he found Leon.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>The mountain trip</h2>
<p>His Green Glove connections help arrange the overnight stay. After Natalia’s collapse, he confronts her about her responsibility to the students and shares his account of what happened to Paloma’s family.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>A life on Earth</h2>
<p>Monty escaped an Oculus Cradle before the system’s collapse and worked closely with Leon afterward. He stayed on Earth during the Great Upload, living through the difficult years Natalia cannot remember.</p>
<p>Their shared past gives them a deep, sometimes sibling-like affection, but their different experiences make reconnection painful as well as comforting. The exact sequence of their early meetings remains a draft detail.</p>
</section>
