<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>Bioluminescent traceries run over Lena’s body. Ihor appears younger than she does, has blue lips, and remains quiet. They ride specialized mechanisms joined by a cord, moving together while Lena speaks.</p>
<h2>Outlook and relationships</h2>
<p>Lena is brilliant and willing to set ethics aside. She and Ihor align with Arcturus’s faith in long-lifer technology. Julia was once a friend and rival, but they are now estranged.</p>
<h2>At Leon’s funeral</h2>
<p>Lena argues for larger technological solutions and prioritizes survival amid worsening TDS. She tells the gathering that she and her father are in agreement.</p>
