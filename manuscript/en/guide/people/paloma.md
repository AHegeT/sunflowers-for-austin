<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Paloma is curious, outspoken, and interested in practical experiments. She is not easily satisfied by explanations that leave her with nothing to do.</p>

<h2>Appearance</h2>
<p>A round, expressive face, dark eyes, sun-kissed skin, and thick, curly black hair, often held back with a colorful bandana. Her imagined captain’s uniform includes cargo shorts and a faded denim vest; at school she can retreat into an anonymous hoodie.</p>
<h2>Personality and skills</h2>
<p>Paloma notices systems, facts, and inconsistencies. She is a practical thinker who enjoys technology and adventure manga, particularly One Piece, and uses the idea of a pirate crew to imagine a place to belong.</p>
<p>She finds hints and indirect communication difficult, takes people literally, and uses headphones and sarcasm as protection. Her apparent toughness coexists with an earnest wish for friendship.</p>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>At school</h2>
<p>After challenging Natalia in class, she apologizes on the stairs. She hoped joining the others would stop them from picking on her.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Working with Natalia</h2>
<p>She joins <a href="natalia.html">Natalia</a> in investigating Leon’s work. Together they discover the egg.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Protecting the egg</h2>
<p>She helps Natalia infiltrate the funeral, recruits Julia’s help, and confronts Arcturus. The creature that hatches becomes <a href="codi.html">Codi</a>.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>Family and the mountain trip</h2>
<p>Paloma believes her parents and brother are still sleeping. Monty tells Natalia that her brother died fighting a wildfire and her parents are awake but badly affected. Paloma has not been told this account.</p><p>Her wish to contact the sleepers helps drive the Codex experiments.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-reference-section">
<h2>The haptic glove</h2>
<p>She developed a glove and receiver with her brother and a former friend. Hand signs and custom movements can become text messages; haptic receivers let users communicate through learned patterns of touch. The glove reflects her preference for making a useful system rather than accepting a frustrating one.</p>
<h2>What she hopes to understand</h2>
<p>The Continuum mystery is personal to her: she wants her family back and hopes understanding what happened can help them. Her desire for a crew is also a desire for a dependable family.</p>
</section>
