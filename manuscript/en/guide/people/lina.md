<p>She is practical and direct, keeping everyday life going while Natalia withdraws into her room.</p>

<p>Sweet, tough, and stubborn, Lina combines care with a refusal to let Natalia abandon everyday responsibilities. Her concern comes through meals, reminders, and practical expectations rather than grand speeches.</p>
