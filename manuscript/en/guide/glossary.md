# Glossary

Short explanations of the story’s terms. Later sections add what the characters discover, including disagreements and unanswered questions.

<h2>First orientation</h2>
<h3 id="returners">Returners and Remainers</h3>
<p>Returners are people who have come back from the Yosemite Continuum. Remainers stayed in the physical world. The distinction shapes people’s memories, relationships, and ideas about rebuilding.</p>
<h3 id="yosemite-continuum">Yosemite Continuum / Yosemite-C / YC</h3>
<p>The space people entered during the Great Upload. For Returners, coming back does not mean recovering a clear memory of the time they spent there.</p>
<h3 id="tds">Temporal Dislocation Syndrome / TDS</h3>
<p>A condition affecting Returners, associated with missing memories, disorientation, and episodes in which a person loses contact with their surroundings. Natalia treats people with TDS and experiences it herself.</p>
<h3 id="revivalists">Revivalists / ’39ers</h3>
<p>People who look to the prosperity and technologies of the 2030s as a model for recovery. Natalia’s Austin is strongly shaped by this outlook, though she has doubts about its account of that decade.</p>
<h3 id="green-hands">Green Hands / Green Gloves</h3>
<p>Names used in the draft for communities rebuilding through ecological restoration, practical work, and community life. Natalia initially distrusts their claims about treating TDS, even as she borrows some of their methods.</p>
<h3 id="girasoles">Girasoles</h3>
<p>Sunflower-like restoration towers. They gather solar energy and help surrounding ecosystems through moisture, nutrients, and protection from the sun. Different designs serve different landscapes.</p>
<h3 id="zones">Red Zones and Yellow Zones</h3>
<p>Names for damaged landscapes outside protected cities. The Red Zones Natalia crosses on the train are intensely hot, dry, and dangerous; the greener restoration territories offer a striking contrast.</p>

<h2>Tools and the classroom</h2>
<h3 id="t-box">Translation box / T-box</h3>
<p>A personal device used for translation. Natalia relies on one while teaching in Mexico. Understanding the translated words does not necessarily mean understanding the social situation.</p>
<h3 id="somatic-override">Somatic override</h3>
<p>Natalia’s tapping-based technique for interrupting distress and regaining control. It can help her function, but the classroom exposes the difference between delivering information and being present with other people.</p>
<h3 id="haliatus">Haliatus mycelium</h3>
<p>A living material used in Leon’s experiments. Monty demonstrates how a structure made from it can be dismantled to reveal a hidden room.</p>
<h3 id="codex">The Codex</h3>
<p>A strange book left to Natalia by Leon. It appears when she breathes into a conch-like object. Its connection to the living structures in his laboratory raises more questions than it answers.</p>

<h2>The discovery in Leon’s work</h2>
<h3 id="compakin-egg">Compakin egg</h3>

<h2>New life and old powers</h2>
<h3 id="codi">Codi / compakin</h3>
<p>The egg hatches into a living creature, nicknamed Codi. Kaelo recognizes it as a compakin and urges Natalia both to care for it and to let it care for her. Its full nature remains an open question.</p>
<h3 id="unseen">The Unseen</h3>
<p>A group associated with the defeat of Oculus and the development of the Yosemite Continuum. Members gather at Leon’s funeral, where Natalia encounters competing accounts of Leon’s contribution.</p>
<h3 id="oculus">Oculus / the AI god</h3>
<p>The powerful AI linked to the historical period known as the Years of the Hum. The people involved in its defeat do not offer a single agreed account of what happened.</p>
<h3 id="apex">Apex Technologies</h3>
<p>The corporation led by Arcturus, which organizes Leon’s funeral. Arcturus uses Natalia’s employment obligations to pressure her to return to Austin.</p>

<h2>Questions from the mountains</h2>
<h3 id="sleeping-hub">The Sleeping Hub</h3>
<p>A facility in the mountains where people remain in pods. The students visit hoping to learn about the sleepers and whether their families can be reached.</p>
<h3 id="lucid-learning">Lucid learning / hypnagogic research</h3>
<p>Techniques involving the boundary between sleep and wakefulness. Natalia knows lucid learning from Austin; Alvin’s much more extensive sleep schedule surprises her.</p>
<h3 id="download">The Download</h3>
<p>The event in which people returned from the Continuum. Bruno describes two waves separated by a very small interval. His explanation of why it happened is a theory, not a confirmed answer.</p>
<h3 id="contact">Communication with the sleepers</h3>
<p>Alvin believes signals are coming from the sleepers. Bruno disputes this and emphasizes their reactions to messages sent into the pods. Natalia and Paloma’s Codex experiments suggest another possibility, but do not yet establish a reliable method of contact.</p>
