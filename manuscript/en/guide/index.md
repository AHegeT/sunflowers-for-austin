## Reading without spoilers

Use **Show information through** to choose the last chapter you have finished. Profiles and reference pages add details as you progress; later sections remain behind chapter warnings. You can open any warning yourself or choose **All available chapters**.

The **Finished Chapter** button at the end of each story chapter advances the setting. Opening a chapter does not count as finishing it. Your choice is remembered in this browser when storage is available and can be lowered or reset.

Character directories show names and first-appearance chapters. Visiting a profile gives a basic introduction; its later story developments remain behind warnings.
