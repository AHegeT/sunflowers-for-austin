## Reading without spoilers

Use **Show information through** to choose the last chapter you have finished. The glossary and History and places pages keep later sections behind chapter warnings. Character profiles and the world chronology are always fully visible. You can open any warning yourself or choose **All available chapters**.

The **Finished Chapter** button at the end of each story chapter advances the setting. Opening a chapter does not count as finishing it. Your choice is remembered in this browser when storage is available and can be lowered or reset.

Character directories, profiles, and the world chronology do not use chapter gates. Supporting characters are grouped by community, workplace, and faction.
