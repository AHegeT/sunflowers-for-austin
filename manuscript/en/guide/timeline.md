# History and places

A broad orientation rather than a definitive chronology. The manuscript and author notes are still evolving, so this guide avoids pinning unsettled events to exact years.

<details class="story-reveal" data-reveal-after="1">

<div class="story-reveal-body">
<h2>The journey south</h2>
<h3 id="austin">Austin, Republic of Texas</h3>
<p>Natalia’s home and workplace: a busy, protected city with Revivalist buildings, extensive professional routines, and a network she depends on. The train journey reveals the difference between life inside its dome and the damaged country outside.</p>
<h3 id="cuernavaca">Cuernavaca, Mexico</h3>
<p>Leon’s home city and Natalia’s destination. Reports of ecological rebuilding draw people south, but Natalia arrives with expectations shaped by Austin.</p>
<h3 id="restoration">A landscape being restored</h3>
<p>The train passes devastated zones, abandoned remains, and greener territories maintained with restoration technology. Recovery is uneven; a thriving city does not mean the wider world has healed.</p>
</div>

<h2>Teaching after the upheavals</h2>
<h3 id="marymount">Marymount</h3>
<p>The school where Natalia and Monty take over Leon’s classes. Its old convent architecture, courtyards, paper records, and daily routines differ sharply from what Natalia expects.</p>
<h3 id="2030s">The 2030s</h3>
<p>Natalia’s history lessons describe a decade of economic development and severe social unrest. Some remember it as a Gilded Age; others emphasize the Great Decay. That disagreement shapes the world’s later attempts at recovery.</p>
<h3 id="hacienda">Leon’s hacienda and laboratory</h3>
<p>A site of living-material experiments and hidden spaces. Monty shows Natalia the concealed room where he found Leon, explaining why he believed the death was deliberate.</p>
</div>
</details>

<h2>The past gathered at a funeral</h2>
<h3 id="hum">The Years of the Hum</h3>
<p>A period associated with Oculus’s power. At the funeral, members of the Unseen claim credit for ending it while arguing over Leon’s role.</p>
<h3 id="upload">The Great Upload</h3>
<p>The move into the Yosemite Continuum was connected to hopes of giving the physical world time to recover. Its legacy is lived differently by those who stayed and those who returned.</p>
<h3 id="tepoztlan">Tepoztlan</h3>
<p>The setting of Leon’s funeral, organized by Apex. The gathering brings figures from the world’s technological and political past into Natalia and Paloma’s investigation.</p>

<h2>The mountain trip</h2>
<h3 id="chichinautzin">Chichinautzin</h3>
<p>The mountain area containing the Sleeping Hub. Access requires permission and a slow journey along a seldom-used train line.</p>
<h3 id="zempoala">Lagunas de Zempoala</h3>
<p>The students’ overnight destination after the Hub visit, with arrangements made through Monty’s Green Glove connections.</p>
<h3 id="return-waves">The return from the Continuum</h3>
<p>Bruno describes two waves of return rather than a perfectly simultaneous awakening. The very small difference in real-world timing may matter because time inside the Continuum behaves differently. The cause is still unresolved.</p>
