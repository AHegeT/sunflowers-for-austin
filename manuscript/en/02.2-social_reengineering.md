This confirmed her hypothesis and she was now ready to change the group dynamics directly. She assigned projects next, with herself as part of the groups so she would no longer be an authority figure to the group.

The project was to analyze the decade of the 2060s with different perspectives with one single aim: What would they have done had they been in charge.
The groups for the project:
Technological perspective: Paloma, Claus, Nestor
Social perspective: Aimé, Daniela, Santiago
Governance perspective: Shaggy, Valeria, Natalia

They break out into groups and begin to do research. The next day, they are ready to present.

Group 1: Paloma leads the group, with Claus and Nestor excitedly contributing. They talk about the complexity of building sleeping pods from the ground up with the fractured infrastructure of the 2060s. These pods needed to be deployed quickly and be able to support humanity for decades or even centuries. They would have used infrastructure from the Cradles, admitting that they agreed with the course of action of the leaders of the past.

Group 2: Daniela started presenting with confidence about the challenge of organizing people during a global crisis after governments had collapsed in previous decades. She explained the fear people must've felt and how they needed to pretend everything was business as usual. Uploading into the Yosemite-C likely felt like a comforting lie to people, some seeing it as a death sentence. But it was a shared dream in a decade where people had stopped being able to believe in anything. She understands how this dream would rally and unite people who just wanted something to cling on to…she then started to tear up. Instead of judging her, Aimé hugged her and allowed her to fully cry on her shoulder. Santi carried on with the presentation, delivering it in a light-hearted tone. He gave his team's conclusion but then added that he personally would've organized a huge dance party for everyone in the world. That way, people would have something to look forward to instead of fearing the end of the world, because there's always a new day. This made Daniela look up and smile at him.

Group 3: During the research phase, Natalia relied on Valeria to stick to the rules, which Shaggy didn't mind. In fact, he easily took over the lead of the project and Natalia allowed this. He often tried to test her and infuriate her with comments and remarks. He even tested her by asking if she wanted to run out of the classroom to cry, as they were approaching the time limit and were not done. This made Natalia think about using her somatic overrid for a moment, but she decided against it. She now understood the game Shaggy was playing and the role he played.
Their presentation went very smoothly, with Natalia herself giving the introduction, Valeria explaining their rationale, and Shaggy giving the conclusion.
"We think it was a mistake to upload 80% of the population to the Yosemite-C. This shattered families, made things more difficult for the Remainers and allowed for the return to be more chaotic, as there was no backup plan. We especially think no child below the age of 12 should've been uploaded, and that their parents should be made to stay with them".
This hit a nerve with the class. They all had their own story. This is when Natalia understood, some of these children were uploaded. Some of them stayed behind while their parents were uploaded.
She disagreed slightly with the conclusion, but allowed Shaggy to lead the direction of the presentation. He knew his classmates better, and could speak to them better than she ever could.

The next day, she came to something surprising, but not totally unexpected: The students were not seated, but standing next to the desks and speaking to one another. She decided to take the opportunity.
They moved the desks to the walls and sat together on the floor. They talked about their experience being uploaded, or staying behind. 
Turns out Daniela had been uploaded for a year, when she could no longer bear being away from her parents. When they were returned, she didn't feel scared, because her parents came back with her. Valeria was uploaded for 3 years, at age 7. She was actually the youngest in the class.
Nestor and Shaggy were both Remainers, though Shaggy was older. They had stuck with each other through thick and thin but they had only met Paloma, when they started school. Natalia realized they were scared of her, since she was the only other Returner in the group. She posed a challenge not only to their social cohesion, but to their friendship as well.

Then they talked about the people that were left behind, those still in the sleeping pods.
Some were missing their parents, one a grandma. Paloma mentioned her whole family was still sleeping in the pods, parents and brother, but that she didn't mind because that way they couldn't nag her. This made the class laugh. They were finally dropping their guard.

This is when the principal showed up. Directora Mendez looked perfectly polished, with her hair up and pearl earrings. She said she had gotten reports of "mess" within the classroom, but she didn't expect to find students sitting on the floors like street urchins. She said she expected better from Natalia and her Austin methods and that this was not what the Marymount stood for. She told her she would be put on administrative probation effective immediately.
She turned to leave and then turned back after a moment…unless, she could prove herself an upstanding teacher and help out since they were understaffed. She was now to teach math and history, and if she slipped up again and anything like this happened again, she would have to ler her go. She should come pick up her curricula the next morning at the admin office.
Paloma made some slight movements with her hands and the whole class chortled and contained laughter. Directora Mendez glared at them and then left.

Natalia asked her what had happened and Paloma said she said something in their chat group. That Natalia should try reseating the principal on the next train to Austin.
