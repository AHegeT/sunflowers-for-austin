### Speaker 1

Okay, _Sunflowers for Austin_.

  

Compakins are made of a malleable, gelatinous material, allowing them to subtly alter their shape and physical consistency. When Cody wants to blend in, he contracts, shrinks slightly, and takes on the look and texture of a rubbery stuffed animal. He maintains his full weight, becoming denser. Doing this is like holding his breath—he still needs to breathe, so he has to do it very carefully.

  

To avoid being discovered by Sebastian Nikolai, they pretend Cody is Paloma's plushie. To sell the illusion, they build an actual decoy stuffed animal in the lab that closely mirrors Cody's appearance, with the eyes being the primary giveaway. When Sebastian Nikolai questions why they carry a plushie everywhere, Natalia points to psychological literature showing that companion plushies dramatically alleviate Temporal Dislocation Syndrome (TDS) symptoms.

  

Sebastian Nikolai frequently interacts with the plushie, sometimes talking to it directly. In doing so, he often unknowingly talks to Cody himself, allowing Cody to understand him intimately.

  

Sebastian Nikolai operates as a double agent. He pretends to work for Arcturus and the 39ers, but his true loyalty lies with the Union. Their motivations differ:

  

- The Union wants the upload hardware—Leon's device, the Codex (known colloquially as the Grimoire). They reject the Yosemite Continuum and want to burn down the old world to establish an AI-directed order. They monitor signals and know Leon had an upload device, but know nothing about compakins.
    
      
    
- Arcturus cares about regaining access codes to the Yosemite Continuum and views compakins as the ultimate solution. He sends Sebastian Nikolai to investigate compakin development, but withholds key details. Arcturus does not realize Leon's egg has already hatched.
    
      
    

To keep Cody hidden from Sebastian Nikolai initially, Natalia maintains a cover story that she returned because she was pregnant after corresponding with Monty—a former rebel friend who is now trying to live a quiet life. Under her clothes, she conceals Cody against her stomach, swapping him with the decoy plushie when needed and masking his distinct eyes.

  

Eventually, a turning point of mutual trust occurs. Sensing Sebastian Nikolai's primary interest is the lab rather than hunting creatures, Natalia decides to be vulnerable. She admits her pregnancy is a lie. As Sebastian Nikolai looks on, her stomach shifts and Cody emerges, greeting him directly. Sebastian Nikolai comes clean about his initial objective concerning the egg, and an open dynamic begins to form.

  

Sebastian Nikolai integrates into the town's defensive protection force. However, the devastating aerial bombing occurs. Sebastian Nikolai had passed intelligence to hurt his rival, Monty, without anticipating the catastrophic harm to Natalia or the town. Horrified by the fallout, Sebastian Nikolai realizes he enabled the very disaster he was supposed to defend against.

  

In the aftermath, Monty succumbs to unhinged, bloodthirsty rage upon discovering Sebastian Nikolai's double-dealing, violently confronting and beating him. Natalia steps in to heal and ground Monty, pulling him back from his dark impulses.

  

Sebastian Nikolai is shattered and genuinely repentant, but his word is broken. He confesses where the stolen Codex is held, allowing the group to reclaim it before the Union can extract it.

  

In the chaos surrounding the aftermath, Cody is captured and taken. Stripped of Cody's emotional grounding, Natalia hits her "Dark Night of the Soul," experiencing acute TDS and emotional paralysis from the compounded trauma of Sebastian Nikolai's betrayal, Monty's rage, and Cody's abduction.

  

Paloma steps into leadership. Rather than giving up, Paloma encourages Natalia to keep moving forward. They learn to harness the Codex directly to scout trajectories and formulate a plan.

  

This drives the entire group—Natalia, Paloma, Monty, and a repentant Sebastian Nikolai—to stage the major infiltration heist into Austin. During the climax, Sebastian Nikolai proves his genuine redemption by putting himself on the line to help rescue Cody. However, in a final desperate maneuver, Arcturus manages to seize Paloma's developing compakin egg, setting the stage for Book 2.
