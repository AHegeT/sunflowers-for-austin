# Summary

This voice memo captures a dialogue between Paloma and Natalia exploring Paloma's shame around being "too much," overly excited, and intensely reactive:

  

- Paloma admits to pulling back and apologizing because she fears judgment, feels people get uncomfortable when she talks fast or passionately, and wishes she could simply "care less" to be cool and fit in.
    
      
    
- Natalia guides Paloma to reframe her feelings, showing her that caring deeply and noticing things others miss are not flaws. Paloma realizes the true issue is not caring too much, but that getting overly reactive causes her to miss details that matter to her.
    
      
    
- Natalia distinguishes between guilt (breaking one's own internal rules) and shame (breaking external social rules or expectations), validating shame as an evolved signal of missing social data rather than proof of doing something wrong.
    
      
    
- Natalia promises not to shut Paloma down for being "a lot," offering instead to give grounded, honest feedback whenever communication fails, feelings are hurt, or important context is overlooked.
