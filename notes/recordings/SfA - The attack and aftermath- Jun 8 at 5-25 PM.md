# Cleaned Transcript

### Speaker 1

Okay, _Sunflowers for Austin_—let's talk about an upcoming chapter.

  

Cody has been painting this red piece. Cody can sense or see the future, and he often paints both the future and the past. He focuses heavily on the emotional experience of it, and he has been painting this terrible inferno that he doesn't like. He is trying to decipher what this feeling is trying to tell him.

  

Once he figures it out—maybe Nikolai even helps—what happens is that Nikolai tells the Union about the sunflower seeds. Specifically, that the seeds carry the information needed to return to the Yosemite Continuum.

  

Nikolai is basically double-crossing Arcturus. He tells Arcturus, but he also tells the Union because he doesn't see a big problem with playing both sides—it has been working out well for him. This leads to the Union wanting to erase the entire area to gain leverage over Arcturus. They send a terrible war machine to burn the whole area down and rain fire.

  

Luckily, the townspeople have prepared because of Cody's vision. They manage to figure out what it involves, and they prepare the region for an attack. They develop an emergency protocol. Cody had put a date on the painting—or perhaps they deduce it from contextual clues—so they believe they know when it's going to happen. Everyone prepares and braces for that terrible date, but nothing happens.

  

They debate whether the prediction was wrong and whether the preparations were a waste of time. However, one of the matriarchs insists: "No, I have a feeling that something is coming. I think we should remain prepared." Other people complain that it's cumbersome and that they can't live like this forever. They agree to maintain the readiness posture for three months. A whole chapter is devoted to this waiting period where nothing comes of it, and the story moves on.

  

Also during this time, Natalia tempers Monty's desire to lash out. She tells him: "Nothing happened, we need to be careful." Monty wants to launch a preemptive strike, but she calms him down, and he decides to stay.

  

About two chapters later, the betrayal scene happens. Nikolai's betrayal is revealed—it's just a look, and with that, he confirms he has betrayed the party.

  

Right after, sirens start to wail. Natalia and the others take cover.

  

We then jump to a recount of the attack by a survivor: "We managed to avoid mass casualties, but we did not manage to avoid the pain. We added fire retardants to the plants, but they were still heavily damaged. Even with safety drills and preparation protocols, it was still chaotic when it happened, and we weren't able to stay well-organized. Some people still incurred heavy burns."

  

We step away from the immediate chaos and observe the aftermath two months later. Things are no longer idyllic—the land has been scorched, people are rebuilding, and some have lost limbs. The sunflowers were completely lost.

  

Shortly after the attack, Arcturus arrived and took either Natalia or Cody with him. That marks the end of this phase and sets up the final chapter—the point of no return—where they head to Austin. They go not just to get revenge, but to retrieve what was stolen. Now that the sunflowers are gone and Arcturus has lost his way back into the Yosemite Continuum, Cody is his final hope.
