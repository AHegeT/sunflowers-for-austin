### Speaker 1

Okay, let's talk about _Sunflowers for Austin_ and the attack—the Rain of Fire.

  

Cody paints every now and then. He paints his feelings, but he also paints moments from different points in time. Basically, he perceives time as if it were a landscape: they call it the Chronoscape.

  

The way it works is that they can untether themselves from the present moment and scout ahead. It's as if they are viewing the upcoming landscape of the future, and they can also view the past this way. They can only see it based on their surroundings and their own experience. It is not a completely fixed future; rather, it behaves like an elastic fabric that you can pull one way or another, though it has set points that need to happen a certain way. This is called historical inertia. If you have a vision and make a change, you can adjust the future, but you can't really change the fixed points. Technically, this also works backward in time, but that is not something Cody knows how to do.

  

Regarding the Rain of Fire: Cody paints when he is relaxed because that is when he can untether himself. He paints a piece that is entirely red. Paloma sees it and says: "Nice, you're finally using red more!" because she likes that color.

  

Natalia, however, gets a terrible, heavy feeling from it, though she isn't initially sure why she has such a heavy feeling about the painting.

Paloma comes up with the idea to follow the track of the plane. Cody stays behind, attempting to analyze more, scouting ahead with the painting to figure out when and what is actually happening and how to deal with it.

While they are scouting, it is very difficult for Natalia. This is where she sees herself hurting Paloma. She apologizes, but Paloma reassures her: "Come on, of course not—there's no way you could hurt me that way. You should not even worry about it; that's not going to happen. That's probably just what would happen if we didn't stop the flight."

  

While doing temporal dislocation, people can get lost or progress into another TDS tier by getting stuck on a future or previous moment, reliving a loop or a very traumatic moment. For Natalia, it has been really difficult as she sees a lot of her traumatic moments and relives them as though they were happening right now. She tells Paloma she doesn't think she can do it anymore.

  

Still, they find a date and believe they know when it is happening. They warn the town, figure out how to protect themselves from the attack, and prepare.

  

When the date comes, nothing happens. However, the elder insists: "No, no, I do sense something is amiss." Because of that, they keep the preparations in place.
