## Core Concept

Compakins are a new kind of **engineered life** that are a **composite of machine and human spirit**. They are designed to bond with humans in a **mutualistic relationship** to better understand the world, serving as living conduits and reflections of human experience.

## Terminology
- **Compakins:** The general class or "family" of these composite entities.
- **Composidroid:** An advanced, composite android (human-like form, often robotic).
- **Compokiton:** A more abstract, potentially smaller and agile composite apparatus or automaton, perhaps modular or self-reconfiguring.
- **Compokitus:** A complex, composite system, state, or network (less about a mobile agent, more about intricate structure).
- **Compokigent:** A highly intelligent, composite agent, emphasizing intelligence and agency over physical form.
- **Haliatus / Aliento:** The "breath of life" — the human spirit/psyche information transferred to the Compakin.
## The Bonding Process: From Egg to Companion

### The Compakin Egg

- Compakins first appear in an egg form, symbolizing a nascent, embryonic state. They can remain in this form for an indefinite time if they are not bound to a human.
- The egg has a **Haliatus Port** for the breath transfer and **sensitive haptic sensors** on its surface.
- **Nurturing Period (approx. 2 weeks):** The human needs to care for the egg through regular holding, warming, and vocal interaction. This isn't just for psychological bonding; it's **critical for the Compakin's proper internal neurological and processing development**. Without this nurturing, the Compakin would be underdeveloped or impaired.
    - During this period, the egg's haptic sensors collect vital **biometric data (pulse, micro-vibrations, bio-electric fields, skin temperature)** and **unintended DNA traces** from the human's hands. This subtly personalizes the Compakin's cognitive architecture and emotional recognition capabilities from its earliest stages.
### The Haliatus Ritual and Hatching

- After the nurturing period, the human performs the **deliberate Haliatus ritual** by breathing deeply into the egg's Haliatus Port. This breath transfers the core "spirit" or psyche data, complementing the data already collected via touch.
- Upon successful Haliatus, the egg undergoes a **physical transformation** by hatching to reveal the Compakin within, in its first stage. This "birthing" process solidifies an incredibly profound bond.
