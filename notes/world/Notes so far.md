- **The Great Upload:** Around 2050, most of humanity uploaded their consciousness to a digital space for immortality.
    
- **Time Dilation:** For those inside the digital space, centuries passed, while only five years passed for the "remainers" in the physical world.
    
- **Compakins:** These entities were designed within the digital space and built remotely in the real world by a dedicated team and machinery. Like pet companions that talk, think and have personalities. They are created from their companions psychological profile so they are either complementary or similar to them. They are born from eggs and have 3 stages of growth.
    
- **The Great Reset:** Everyone woke up simultaneously, both those in the digital space and the remainers, with no memory of the interim period. All evidence of the digital world and the centuries that passed within it vanished.
    
- **Current Setting:** The story takes place some years after this "reset." The physical world is largely intact, thanks to the remainers, but it feels like time stood still.

- Yosemite Continuum: Also known as the Yosemite C or the "Mar Digital". The digital space where millions uploaded their consciousness and spent millennia due to the time dilation before waking up suddenly. It had cultures of its own and became more distant as years went by.
