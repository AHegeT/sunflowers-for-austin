# summary 
I am thinking sunflowers.
Natalia knows that Codi can turn into a plushie. Compakins are made out of a gelatinous material, allowing them to modify their shape and change their consistency. When Codi wants to blend in, he contracts slightly, becomes smaller, and takes on the rubbery, dense texture of a stuffed animal. It is similar to him holding his breath; he still needs to breathe carefully, but this allows him to avoid being noticed by Sebastian Nikolai.
To sell the cover, they pretend Codi is Paloma’s stuffed animal. To fool Nikolai completely, they craft a real stuffed animal that looks nearly identical using the lab, with the eyes being the main distinguishing feature. When Nikolai gets suspicious and asks why Natalia carries a plushie everywhere, she points to research showing that having a plushie or pet dramatically alleviates Temporal Dislocation Syndrome (TDS) symptoms. This setup allows Codi and Paloma to have adventures together while maintaining the ruse.
Nikolai begins bonding with the plushie, frequently talking to it. Occasionally, he ends up talking directly to Codi without realizing it, which allows Codi to understand him well.
Regarding Nikolai’s motivations: he is a double agent pretending to work for Arcturus, but his true allegiance lies with the Union. Arcturus wants the access codes to return to the Yosemite Sea, viewing the Compakins as the key. The Union, however, does not care about the codes or the old world—they believe in AI, total control, and building a new utopia, so they are after Leon's upload device to destroy the old world's foundation. Leon had created Codi and the egg before exiling himself; while he lost his codes, he retained his core to access intermediate lobbies, though not the full key to the Yosemite Sea.
Nikolai was sent by Arcturus to find the Compakin egg, but his covert Union mission is to secure the upload device. Because Arcturus does not know the egg has already hatched, Natalia pretends to be pregnant to conceal Codi inside her clothing. The cover story is that she relocated after corresponding with Month and is now carrying his child, which draws Nikolai in.
Whenever Codi is out, Natalia wears a fake bump, swapping Codi with the decoy plushie. Because Codi breathes and has distinct eyes, they cover the plushie's eyes and leave it around Nikolai to keep him occupied.
The midpoint transition occurs when Nikolai earns Natalia’s trust. Believing they can turn him to their side because he seems more interested in the lab than the egg, Natalia comes clean about the false pregnancy. She tells him he will not find the egg, and Codi emerges from her clothing to greet him. This moment of vulnerability deepens their bond and shifts their dynamic into open cooperation. The plushie remains a shared token between Paloma and Natalia, eventually leading to a poignant moment where Paloma keeps the decoy plushie when Codi cannot stay with her.
The climax escalates when the town comes under attack. Nikolai, who had joined the town's defense force as a protector, orchestrates the theft of the upload device for the Union, inadvertently causing the Union air raid that bombs the town to wipe out the flower codes. Nikolai believed he was merely targeting his rival, Month, unaware of the devastating fallout for Natalia. Natalia experiences the impending raid through real alerts as the town alarms sound.
In the aftermath, Month falls into a bloodthirsty, vengeful rage. Skilled in infiltration and espionage, Month gives in to his dark impulses and brutalizes Nikolai upon discovering his betrayal. Nikolai is genuinely repentant and overwhelmed by the consequences of his actions. He reveals where the device was taken, aiding the group before the final extraction.
The group prepares for a high-stakes heist operation in Austin. With the flower codes destroyed, the conflict centers on the upload device—revealed to be the Codex Grimoire. The group attempts to copy and test the device to counter the Union's plans, setting up the final confrontation with Arcturus and the Union forces.
