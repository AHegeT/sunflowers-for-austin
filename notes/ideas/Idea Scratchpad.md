	- Natalia’s Internal Conflict (The Hamartia)
		- The Flaw: Natalia’s fatal flaw (hamartia) is defined as a desire to understand the "enemy" so deeply that she risks failing to stand by her own principles.
		- The Setup: She initially fails as a teacher because she relies on a rigid, outdated curriculum that doesn't fit the fractured reality of her students.
		- The Low Point: The students, who are a mix of different systems and trauma backgrounds, essentially bully her, making her want to quit her search for Leon's truth.
	- The Classroom: Returned vs. Remainers
		- The chapter highlights a significant societal friction between those who stayed on Earth and those who skipped the "Lost Decade".
		- Mental vs. Chronological Age: The classroom is chaotic because "Returned" students might be 23 chronologically but possess the mental maturity of 13-year-olds because they were uploaded as children.
		- The "Handicapped" Stigma: The school has a specific section for the Returned, who are often unfairly viewed as mentally handicapped simply because they missed ten years of developmental life.
		- Ideological Clash: The "Returned" staff and leadership want to return to the sterile safety of the Oculus era, while the "Remainer" students—who lived through the climate hardships and the First Spring—fiercely resist that regression.
	- Paloma and the Turning Point
		- First Impression: Paloma is initially quiet and observant, testing Natalia to see if her teacher is "full of bullshit.".
		- The Insight: Instead of being a passive student, Paloma provides Natalia with a critical insight that sparks Natalia’s interest in radical education.
		- Research: This prompts Natalia to study historical educators like Dewey, Makarenko, and Tudor-Demetra to find a way to "learn from the past" and adapt the Revivalist model to the current world.
		- The Hype Moment: Natalia proposes "Field Expeditions" instead of stuffy lectures. When the "stuffy" principal (a Returned struggling to adjust) tries to shut her down, Paloma stands up for Natalia, cementing their bond.
	- The Shadow of Leon
	- The Investigation: Amidst the school drama, Natalia continues investigating Leon’s suspicious "suicide.".
	- The Ghostly Feeling: Natalia feels she is being followed by an ever-present figure. While she initially feels like it might be Leon's ghost or a threat, it is revealed to be different people watching her, including Paloma, who is intrigued by her new teacher.
