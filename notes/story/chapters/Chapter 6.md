Scene
Leon's Greenhouse
Smell: Acrylic paint, wet earth

The ambience is calm and comforting. Natalia is grading some papers, Paloma dismantling one of the drones painstakingly and Codi rolling on butcher paper, occasionally stopping to cover parts of his body with more paint.

N- I was not expecting these papers to be...decent? Some of them take strange turns, but they're all aiming for different things. If I follow along, they can be quite captivating.
P- So you're saying you were wrong???
N- That's not it! I'm just saying...I might have judged them prematurely.
C- Ha! Embarrassment.
N- I know embarrassment, Codi, it's one of the primary emotions, thank you.
P- Codi could be a living lying detector. It's funny how you adults come up with all these excuses to get over the simple facts of life.
N- Perhaps...I do think things would be easier if we could share how we feel without judgment. But I wouldn't want Codi yelling how I feel with a patient!
C- That's contemplation!
P- I love it, it's like he's learning his ABCs but with naming emotions.
N- I wish some of these students knew their ABCs better, who writes "akseptans" with a k, s and...just like this?
P- Pfff, ridiculuous!
N- Wait, is this...yours, Paloma??
P- What!
C- Embarrassment!
Natalia and Codi laugh, while Paloma huffs and puffs.
P- Well, we'll see whose embarrassed when Mister /Nicolai/ gets here
She teases her with lovey-dovey noises.
N- There's nothing to be embarrassed about there. He's just someone from my past.
P- You said he was a childhood friend. You don't seem to have many freinds.
N- It's from back when, when we were living in the Cradles, aaages ago.
P- Right, the weird communes of the AI God.
N- Yeah, they were just...optimized communities. Nicolai was a friend of a friend, we grew up with the same friend group.
C- Sadness?
Codi says in a concerned tone, he stops painting and gets closer to Natalia.
N- Ah, it's not sadness, Codi. It's just...old memories.
P- It seems to be more than that...were you guys close?
N- Yeah...it was my "chosen family". Oculus had this peculiar idea of orchestrating whimsical or serendipitous events to get you to notice people you were algorithmically compatible with. That's how I met Jan. We very quickly became a couple, and Nicolai was his brother. Baby brother. He would tease me to no end, trying to seem tough for Jan. But he was also...very endearing. He could captivate a whole room with his jokes and his speeches. We went on many trips together and eventually he met Max, his life partner. They were an even worse pain in the butt together, always trying to outcompete everyone on how smart they were, especially each other. Jan and Max became best friends and that would leave me stuck with Nicolai most of the time. I called him Sebby back then, since Max also called Jan Nicolai.
P- You guys have like /history/ together.
C- Roman?
N- Haha, that's the empire, Codi. I think you mean "romance" and...yes, I started to develop feelings for Sebby.
P- So I was right! You do want to /smoooch/ his pretty face!
Natalia stares longingly at the window, lost in thought.
N- I...don't know.
C- Pain...
Natalia turns back at Codi and smiles.
N- No, not at all. I'm sorry.
Her hand drifts up to her neck automatically. She doesn't even realize she’s doing it. Her index and middle finger find the vagus nerve, just below the jawline. _Tap. Tap. Pause._ It is a rhythmic, mechanical stimulus, a somatic reset code she learned a lifetime ago to down-regulate the parasympathetic nervous system.

_Tap. Tap. Pause._

Codi stares at her. His head tilts, his flower flashing a chaotic strobe of Blue-Grey-White. He is trying to process an input that isn't there because she is manually deleting it.
N- It's all fine. Like I said, an old friend.
C- That...what?
N- What what, Codi?
P- Yeah, what is it, Codikun?
C- I...I...ERROR
P- Well, now it's my turn to guess that he's feeling confused. Did you do something, Natalia?
N- No? We were just talking...
C- Confusion, confusion...sad, empty, sad, empty...ERROR
Codi’s eyes widen unnaturally. The processing loop of Natalia’s suppressed emotion has nowhere to go. It backs up inside him until it physically erupts.
P- Now I really think you did something, Natalia.
N- Could it be the memories? Maybe he's absorbing them? Codi, are you okay? Breathe with me.
Natalia starts showing a breathing exercise but Codi is not responding well, seemingly in an anxious trance. Paloma gets up, unsure of how to help. Natalia rushed to his side and tries to calm him down by placing her hand on his body.

A beam of pure, white data-light bursts from Codi’s eyes, slamming directly into Natalia’s gaze.

The greenhouse vanishes.

Natalia is nowhere. She has no hands, no feet. She is a point of consciousness floating in a desolate, oxidized red landscape. The sky is choked with dust.
_Dread._

The emotion hits her, but without a body to process it, it feels like a mathematical fact.
"TDS attack. Anchor, anchor...I need to find my way back".
Her training kicks in. She scans the horizon frantically. The red dust is alien, shifting. She needs a constant. There—a dead, calcified twig of a tree jutting out of the red earth.
She locks her focus on the twig.

She tries to find something to anchor her gaze and finds a dead twig tree. She stares at it with laser focus, as the clouds around her start moving faster and faster. The sun comes out, blocked by the clouds and then goes away. Night comes and goes, with days going faster and faster.
"I'm so far away, I need to get back."
The passage of days starts speeding up faster and faster, until more dead plants start appearing and disappearing. As time keeps moving, some of them start showing some green and the landscape seems to come to life in an instant. Some debris around them takes shape and starts making out a building.
"I know where I am!"
Time starts to slow down. The movement of people. Ghosts of the past, flickering in and out. She slows the feed down until the blurs resolve into figures. Two kids. They are standing next to the tree; now alive, covered in leaves.
Time starts to slow down and the movement of many people can be seen. Coming and going about their daily life. Until it almost stops at one night when two kids where seen standing next to the tree, only to go their separate ways.
"Too far...I need to go back"
Time stops and then starts to move in the other direction. With the kids now coming from where they came, to meet at the tree. They lay next to the tree, his back on it and hers on his chest.
Time stops completely as Natalia fixates on that moment. It is a younger version of her... sitting against the tree trunk. And a boy with a sharp smile is lying with his head in her lap.
Sebby.
Then, she feels it. A weight.

Not a memory of weight, but _actual_ mass pressing down on where her shoulders should be. A tether, pulling her down from the ether and pinning her to a physical reality.

She gasps. Her lungs fill with air. Her hands materialize; her arms, her legs. She looks up at the weight on her shoulders and sees Codi hanging on her back. She closes her eyes and opens them again to find herself back at the greenhouse.

C- Sorry, sorry! Sorry, Talia!
Codi cries as he is perched on her shoulders, caressing her face with her snout. Paloma is holding her, also distraught. 
N- Nothing to worry about, I'm fine. Just a TDS attack.
P- That's a TDS attack?
N- Yeah, a rather strong one...I was beginning to feel suspicious that I hadn't had any in a while. Well, there goes my record!
P- Your eyes were shining.
N- Oh? What color? Would you say it was an intense shine?
P- I...what?
N- Actually, how long has it been? Did I fall to the ground or did you get me down for safety? Did I say anything during the episode?
P- Natalia, what are you talking about? This was scary!
N- Yes, yes. Now let me get my notebook so I can write all of this down. Try to record all the notes on your phone in the meantime!
Natalia rushes out, leaving a confused Paloma and Codi behind.
C- Em...barrassment?
