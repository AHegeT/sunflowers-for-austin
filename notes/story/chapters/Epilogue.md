After Natalia and Arcturus come back from the YC, they experience Memory Decay. They come back having just experienced 40 years of a happy life where they got married and even had grandchildren.

|**Character**|**Perspective on the "Missing 40 Years"**|**Psychological Impact**|
|---|---|---|
|**Natalia**|Sees Paloma as a "child from her past" rather than a current student.|Feels the crushing weight of having "abandoned" her crew for a lifetime.|
|**Arcturus**|Clings to the memories because it was the only time he was truly "kind" and loved.|Becomes even more "bullish" and controlling as the feeling of that love slips away.|
|**Paloma**|Has no context for the 40 years; only saw Natalia disappear into a pod for minutes.|Views Natalia’s sudden bond with Arcturus as a nonsensical, sudden betrayal.|

# Julia and Paloma Draft
J- You want to stop a war, child?

Her words still lingered in Paloma's head. She felt so confident and so strong when she agreed to come with her. But now...the image of Natalia going into the Yosemite-C kept playing over and over again. It would be instantaneous and also eternal, like the time she kept waiting for her to come back, hoping...
The punch Arcturus laid in her gut still ached, now more than before. Everything was starting to come back.

P- ...How do you kill...your feelings?

Julia turns to her immediately, then turns back to making their meal.

J- People better than me have tried. I've yet to hear of someone who's been successful.
P- I don't want to feel anymore. I don't want to remember. I just want to...
J- Leave it behind?
P- Yes.
J- You will. Eventually. For now you have to be haunted by it.
P- But I don't want to! It wasn't my fault!!
J- No, child. It was not...the world is often cruel with those who are least at fault. That is why you and I are here.
P- I just want to...not have to deal with this...bullshit.
J- It won't make sense for now. It's okay to numb, to run from it...just for now. It will be a long time before you understand.
P- And what do I do meanwhile?
J- You keep going, you let it hurt and take breaks when it's too much. 
P- She was my best friend...she was my family...
J- ...
P- I want to forget her. I want to forget everything about her.
J- ...
P- Everything.
J- I will not stop you. Just make sure you don't forget who you are. In losing others, we often lose a part of ourselves.
P- But that is not fair, why does she get to destroy all the things that I love? She was supposed to protect me...
J- And to betray someone we love, we first have to betray ourselves. A dark road awaits her...but you don't have to wait for her to come back. You are safe now.
P- ...
J- You can talk to me about these things. I will not try to erase your pain nor to heal it before its time. But I want you to remember that war tears families apart, it chips away at people's souls. Do not lose sight of why we're here. If you can, spare others of the hurt that you've been subjected to.
P- Or make others pay for the hurt they put people through.
J- ...

# Last Arcturus and Nat Scene Draft

Arcturus and Natalia are laying in bed, he lays his head on her chest. His face is peaceful and happy, with his eyes closed. Hers is troubled, her eyes open and staring at the ceiling.
Arcturus's fishtank is glowing in the dark at a distance, devoid of any fish.

N- I think I made a mistake
A- Everything will be okay, Nat. I'm certain now.
N- ...

Natalia continues to lie awake. She holds Artcurus's head lovingly and motions that she's getting up. His eyes open, startled.

N- Rus...I need to talk to you.
A- What is it? Is something wrong?
N- I need to go, Rus.
A- What? But...no...
N- I think I've made the biggest mistake of my life.
A- No, Nat, you're wrong. We were happy. We were--
N- I left Paloma alone, Arcturus. I left her alone all this time.
A- And it was fine. We've atoned for our mistakes, remember, we even made that ceremony all those years ago. You moved on. You don't need her to forgive you.
N- No, my love. What I did was wrong. I need to tell her, I need to make it up to her, if it's even possible, or at least try...
A- Then go. Get out.
N- Rus?
A- I said GET OUT OF HERE! LEAVE!! I want you OUT!!!

Natalia gets up immediately, confused and terrified. She gathers whatever things she can and scurries out of the bedroom as Arcturus keeps yelling.

A- You traitor! Traitor!! YOU TRAITOR!!!
