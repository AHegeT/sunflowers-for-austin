		- Paloma uses her skills to make it realistic
	- Using Natalia's social engineering to move around

- Avoiding Arcturus
	- Arcturus takes interest in Natalia and starts keeping an eye
	- The more they move around, the more interested he is and the more intently he follows
	- Trying to lose him more and more
	- Eventually it's clear he's following them and they use the only person they think can distract him

- Julia + Arcturus fight
	- Arcturus is lured into a room with his mother, Julia
	- They haven't talked in many years and their resentment is palpable
	- Arcturus tries to be stoic but Julia easily presses his buttons, even if she has good intentions
	- Exposition about their relationship, Arcturus's motivation and trying to escape her shadow
		- Explaining his visions of the red wasteland

- The unseen in the lab
	- They manage to make it into the lab, where they find many of the Unseen gathered
	- They discuss politics and Leon's involvement
	- They reveal they know about why people were offloaded
	- Kaelo is present, there is tension between all of them
	- We're introduced to them all
	- They manage to find what they came for and slip by almost unnoticed
		- One of the Unseen takes note
- Meeting Julia
	- As they're leaving, Julia intercepts them
	- Paloma might stay away, she's wary of Julia
	- Natalia is curious and open
	- Natalia opens up about how sad it is that most people remember Leon negatively, even if he doesn't remember him well
	- Julia suggests she sings of what he meant to her, to speak from the heart and not the mind
		- She won't get another chance to honor his memory
		- She might be the only person who knew that part of him, nobody might agree, but nobody is only one thing
	- Julia gives advice on faking a pregnancy
		- Most people won't be able to tell, but if you were pregnant, remember you've been carrying this for months now. You should be compensating for the weight.
- Kathy's Song
	- Natalia decides to sing in honor of Leon's memory, a song he used to like
	- Asks the man playing the guitar if he knows Kathy's Song, he's like "Simon and Garfunkel?"
	- Se sings lovely and people listen
	- She realizes Leon didn't kill himself, the egg start to stir
- Arcturus Confrontation
	- Natalia seeks out Paloma and she tells her. She thinks Leon was murdered
	- Arcturus goes directly to her and challenges her
	- Tensions escalate and he's triggered by her vulnerability, leaving his company and choosing Leon. He tries to goad her
	- He backs her against the wall, trying to intimidate her. She's unflinching but the egg is hatching
	- Paloma hits him in the groin and they take the chance to escape
- The egg hatches
	- They escape the funeral and the egg hatches in the middle of the street
	- Paloma stays behind to slow them down
	- Arcturus's agents pin her down, but Kaelo comes in and tells them to disband, he protects Natalia and tells them if they harass her, they will have to deal with him
	- Codi is born and Kaelo knows of compakins
	- He parts and leaves some wisdom behind
