[[Chapter 4]]


Codi, Natalia and Paloma arrive at the Tinaguis of Wires.
N- What did you say it's called, again?
P- A Cantor B-90, or the Coleman HX, if they have it. It's very rare, but this place carries really good stuff at times.
N- That sounds rare and expensive...
P- Expensive? Oh, I thought you had that Austin money...
N- A little, I'm just a corporate therapist and a schoolteacher now, at that.
P- Oh...that's...going to throw a wrench in our plans.
N- It'll be fine, we'll just haggle! I'm an expert negotiator! Did I tell you I was champion of my poker league?
P- You did not...Oh!
C- The /Cradle/ poker league.
P- Oh...
They walk around the Tianguis, Codi wearing the "Service Drone" vest, with the Dog part crossed and written over. He is doing his best at playing the part, but people are staring.
P- Oh no, I think they're noticing Codi.
C- On no, am I doing it wrong? Bark, bark!
Everyone turns and stares in disbelief at him. The trio freezes. A young man approaches.
Man- What...is this thing?
P- A robot with [tech jargon] sensors! It scanned the area and noticed stray dogs usually abound! It's trying to describe what they would sound like! I mean, the noise they would make!
C- Bark, right! Bark!
N- It's an emotional support robot. My friend here underwent a traumatic episode with a dog attack and it helps her adjust to going out in public little by little. Like exposure therapy!
Man- Oh...so he's a dog? I thought it was a robot crocodile!
P- You mean alligator, crocodiles have a V-shaped snout.
Man- Yeah, that. Can I pet it?
All together, even Codi- No!
Man- Relax, relax. Man, I don't know which one of you is more likely to bite my hand off.

They keep walking in relief. Natalia notices Codi's flower is changing color a multitude of times as they walk by.
N- Everything okay, Codi?
C- There's so many people...cold, hot, heavy...
P- Heavy? Is the vest too heavy for you?
C- No, I feel...I don't know what this is. Like ramen...
P- So, good!
C- No, or...maybe?
N- I think he's overwhelmed with so many people. You're going to have to tune them out, Codi. You can't be feeling what others are feeling all the time, it's not healthy, trust me.
C- Mmmrgh.
N- Yeah, I think that's my cue. Paloma, do you mind getting the...flux capacitor or whatever yourself?
P- But--I thought you were going to help--assist me.
N- You know exactly what you need. Just ask for it, if they give you a price that's too high, ask why and check if they're bluffing or if it's a real constraint. Call me if it's real. I'll stay behind to give Codi some calming energy.

Paloma walks along to the Capitan Tuercas stall. She always orders online and just waits if the part is not available, but this time, she needs to try the heat dampener for Codi herself, she needs to test it. So she takes a breath in, widens her shoulders and prepares to deepen her voice. She walks to the woman on the other side of the counter.
P- I hear this place carries the Coleman HX.
Woman- Coleman HX? Meaning...?
P- Ah, please connect me to the manager, I wish to order some valuable merchandise.
Woman- The manager? And why would that be?
P- To explain my rare and sophisticated request.
Woman- You're never going to guess who you're talking to, little girl.
P- I do dare to guess! I am speaking to the wife of the manager!
Woman- Wrong.
P- Oh...
Paloma's stance falters as the woman looms closer and over her.
Woman- And I was asking if you wanted a Coleman HX for heat dispersion or heat regulation. They are not the same, you know? But it's a novice mistake to make.
Paloma- Novice?! You barely have any merchandise here! I almost mistook it for a gift store! Any fridge magnets you want to sell to me?
Woman- As a matter of fact, we do sell fridge magnets...the Tien-Gao 5000!
Paloma- The super magnets that both conduct and cool down!?
Woman- Precisely! And I'm selling them to you for just 30 thousand credits!!
P- 30 thousand?!
Woman- A special price just for you, my dear!! Hahaha!
Paloma- Ahhh!!!
Paloma runs out screaming from the store and back to where Codi and Natalia were.

P- I messed up! I just ruined our chances, they're hiking the prices by 100x!
N- How did you manage that in such little time?
P- I don't know! I was just trying to do what you told me to: Act tough, call their bluff and imply I can get their wares for dirt cheap!
N- That's...not even close to what I was saying.
C- Mmmm...
Codi seems relaxed, with a bright purple flower on his back.

N- This isn't bothering you?
C- Nope, this is nice and comfortable fear. It "tastes" like cherries!
N- Huh, well, at least it's not too much for you. Tell you what, Paloma, why don't you and Codi come with me while I go haggle with her? I'm sure it's nothing serious. Just make sure to stay close to Codi so he can soak up in your absolute fear of this regular merchant?
P- Uh-huh!

The three walk to the stall and are met by a large but friendly woman.
Woman- Did you come to take advantage of the fantastic offer I made for you, little girl?
N- She said you're hiking the prices by 100x? Why is that?
Woman- Because she is the most indecent customer I've had in a long time!
Codi's flower starts glowing yellow.
N- In a long time, huh? When was the last time something like this happened?
P- Probably when computers were still the size of her hips!
Paloma chimes in with an insult before returning to hide behind the pillar of cables, next to Codi, whose flower turns pruple again.
Woman- Haha, actually, it was yesterday, when I had a little impertinent girl like you for breakfast!
Codi's flower turns yellow again.
P- Ahhh!!
N- Listen, we just want the part and then we'll be on our way. We can get you many customers if you give us a good deal. How much is the part normally?
Woman- If the little girl wants it, the little girl has to buy it.
N- Come on Paloma, and remember, just ask questions.
Woman- Yes, yess...
Paloma walks in nervously, she tries to puff out her chest but it deflates instantly from the fear of confronting this hag of a woman who is threatening to eat her for breakfast. She then notices Codi's flower is still a bright yellow, even despite her fear.
P- Codi? Is that right? Does it taste like...macadamia nuts?
C- Exactly! With a slight tint of cherry!
Paloma seems to have a realization and she faces the woman.
P- You're not angry at me? You're...happy?
Woman- I'm delighted! You're the most fun customer I've had in years! You remind me of my little granddaughter, though, with that irreverence! Everyone here treats me as just Doña Tuercas, they all come in trying to suck up to me when they can't even understand the complexities of what I sell!

# AI ending
**\[ ...Picking up after Paloma runs back to the group... \]**

"She's hiking the prices!" Paloma hissed, hiding behind Natalia. "She wants thirty thousand!"

"Thirty thousand?" Natalia frowned, doing the mental math. "That’s... steep, but within my discretionary budget. I suppose for artisanal modifications, one must pay a premium."

"No!" Paloma looked at her like she was insane. "That's robbery! You can't just pay it!"

"Watch me," Natalia said, adjusting her blazer. "Stay close to Codi. He’s spiking."

Codi was vibrating in his service vest, his eyes darting around the chaotic stall. _“Loud. Needles. Static. Too much flavor!”_

Natalia didn't lecture him this time. She moved seamlessly into "Handler Mode." She stepped between Codi and the crowd, creating a physical buffer, and placed a hand firmly on the back of his neck—not tapping, just holding. A grounding anchor. Codi leaned into her leg, his vibrations smoothing out into a low hum.

"I've got him," Natalia said calmly. "You get the part. Go."

They approached the stall. Doña Tuercas loomed over the counter, eating a bag of chili-covered sunflower seeds. She spat a shell out; it pinged off a pristine, vintage motherboard.

"Back for more?" Tuercas boomed, eyeing Paloma. "Or did you bring your mother to fight your battles?"

"I am her associate," Natalia said, projecting her best Corporate Austin voice. She reached for her datapad. "And we are prepared to meet your quote of thirty thousand. Transferring now."

Doña Tuercas stopped chewing. Her eyes went wide. She looked at the datapad, then at Natalia, like she was holding a live grenade.

Paloma saw the look. She looked at Codi. Codi was peeking out from behind Natalia’s leg. His flower wasn't Purple (Fear). It was **Neon Orange**. _"Papaya,"_ Codi whispered. _"Sweet. Sticky. Surprise."_

_Surprise?_ Paloma looked at Tuercas. Why was she surprised to get paid? Unless...

"Stop!" Paloma slammed her hand down on Natalia’s datapad, blocking the transfer.

"Paloma?" Natalia sighed. "I’m handling it."

"No, you're not," Paloma snapped. She turned to Tuercas. "Thirty thousand _what_, Doña?"

Tuercas grinned, a gold tooth flashing. "Whatever is on the tag, _niña_."

Paloma grabbed the Coleman HX. She flipped the tag over. It read: **30k J**.

"Joules," Paloma said, exhaling. "Not Credits. Joules. Energy trade."

"Same thing to a rich tourist," Tuercas laughed, reaching for the pad again. "I accept donations."

"It is _not_ the same thing!" Paloma shoved the part back. "30k Joules is... what, three hours of solar output? That's garbage currency. You're charging us in trash!"

"It's a trash market!" Tuercas roared, delighted. "If you want to pay in Austin Credits, the price is five hundred. Firm."

"Five hundred credits?!" Natalia started to nod. "That sounds reas—"

"No!" Paloma interrupted again, finding her rhythm. "Five hundred is for mint condition. Look at this." She pointed a greasy finger at the casing. "The thermal paste is dried out. I have to re-apply it myself. And the coil is third-generation salvage."

Tuercas narrowed her eyes. The theatrical menace dropped, replaced by a sharp, calculating look. She wasn't looking at a scared kid anymore; she was looking at a technician.

"The coil is vintage," Tuercas countered. "Copper-wound. Better than the synthetic crap you kids use."

"Copper heats up faster," Paloma shot back. "I'll give you two hundred credits. And I'll fix the wiring on your fan over there so you stop sweating through your shirt."

Tuercas blinked. She looked at the rattling, broken fan in the corner of her stall. Then she looked at Paloma. A slow, genuine smile spread across her face.

"Two-fifty," Tuercas said. "And you fix the fan right now."

"Deal," Paloma said.

As Paloma went to work on the fan, Natalia leaned against the counter, stroking Codi’s head. Codi was humming a soft, content melody.

"You saved us," Natalia whispered to Paloma’s back. "Good catch on the currency."

Paloma smirked, twisting a wire. "I saved you twenty-nine thousand, five hundred credits. You were about to pay the 'Idiot Tax'."

"I was about to pay for _convenience_," Natalia corrected, though she looked impressed. "But I suppose... this is more fun."

"Fun?" Codi chirped, his flower turning a soft, happy yellow. _"Popcorn!"_
