# Main Characters
### Natalia Sunday

#### **Basic Information**

- **Full Name:** Natalia Sunday
- **Age:** 37, born 2036
- **Occupation (Current):** High School Teacher (Philosophy/Ethics/Contemporary History) at Marymount Cuernavaca.
- **Occupation (Former):** Corporate Psychologist in Austin, TX.
- **Hometown:** Austin. Born in Victoria, TX.
#### Physical Description

Natalia is a woman who wears her exhaustion like a well-tailored, but fraying, suit of armor. She has light brown skin and dark brown hair, which she almost always contains in a tight, tired bun—a style chosen more for efficiency than effect. Her features are sharp and intelligent, dominated by perceptive brown eyes that are often guarded or downcast. Her wardrobe is a monochromatic relic of her past life in Austin: structured blazers, functional trousers, and severe blouses in shades of black and charcoal. The clothes are high-quality but show subtle signs of wear, mirroring her own state. She is at a low point, and her rigid, closed-off posture is less about power and more about simply holding herself together.

#### Personality

**Fictional Comparison:** Jessica Jones

Natalia is a brilliant analyst trapped under the weight of her own trauma. As one of the awakened who survived the Yosemite Continuum collapse with a five-year memory gap, she is deeply cynical and disillusioned with the world. She wields a dry, biting sarcasm as her primary defense mechanism, creating an intellectual and emotional wall to keep others at a distance.

Though she is a trained psychologist, she is completely unable to process her own pain, choosing instead to suppress it. She is guarded, pragmatic, and initially resistant to any form of emotional vulnerability or whimsical fantasy, as shown by her reactions to Paloma's "pirate crew." Beneath the cynical armor, however, is a person with a powerful conscience and a deeply buried capacity for fierce loyalty and protection. She doesn't want to be a hero; she just wants to be left alone, but her sense of responsibility—to her late mentor and to the vulnerable people she lets past her guard—compels her to act.

She is also very empathetic and seeks to understand others. She is very good at building alliances and seeing things from another's point of view, when she is at her best.
She observes people. She sees posture, dilation of pupils, hierarchy, and defense mechanisms.

- Her Flaw: She over-analyzes. She looks for "Deep Psychological Reasons" (Trauma? Insecurity? Power play?) when the answer is often mundane (Hunger? Bathroom?). She is "High Context" but lacks the local data to interpret it correctly in Cuernavaca.
- **Social logic:** Natalia is a **Behavioral Analyst**. She is excellent at reading intent and managing power dynamics (Austin style).
    
- **The Blind Spot:** She is helpless with the physical world. She can't fix a toaster, and she often forgets her own biological needs (eating, resting) because she is too busy analyzing her own thoughts.

- Fearing for her safety and needing cover, she took a teaching position at Marymount Cuernavaca, the only connection she had left to her mentor's world.
- She was born in the middle of the Gilded age and Great Decay. Experiencing deep societal divisions.

- Codi: Her bonded compakin. She initially struggles to care for him but finds deep respect and company with him. They have an unspoken understanding and eventually Codi becomes the driver for her self-care and understanding.

- Montante

#### Abilities
- Deep Empathy and Understanding
- Building Alliances and Negotiation
- Somatic Override
	- A technique where you can separate a person's psyche from their emotions, letting the body handle stress or anxiety without engaging with it. She often uses it with her patients and herself to control TDS symptoms. It's akin to turning a computer "off and on".
#### Character Arc & Objective

- **External Objective:** To understand the device her mentor left her, uncover the truth behind the Yosemite Continuum's collapse, and discover why her mentor was killed.
    
- **Internal Objective:** To move past her trauma and cynicism, dismantle the emotional walls she has built, and learn to trust and connect with others again. Her journey is about shifting from a state of pure, isolated survival to one of active, cooperative purpose, truly earning her title as Commander of a crew she has grown to love.

She represents the Mother, eventually coming to believe we must understand the trauma to move forward.

#### Basic Information
- **Full Name:** Paloma Soliz

- **Occupation:** High School Student at Marymount Cuernavaca.
- **Hometown:** Cuernavaca

#### Physical Description

Paloma’s appearance is a direct reflection of her vibrant inner world. She has a round, open face with large, deeply expressive dark eyes that seem to see more than most. Her sun-kissed skin and the chaotic explosion of thick, curly black hair she often wrangles with a colorful bandana give her an air of constant, playful energy. When "in her element," she wears her self-appointed captain's uniform: practical cargo shorts, a t-shirt featuring an adventure motif, and a faded denim vest over it all. At school, however, she employs camouflage, often disappearing into a simple, anonymous hoodie to quietly observe the world and read her manga in peace.

#### Personality

**Fictional Comparison:** Lilo Pelekai (_Lilo & Stitch_), Anya (Buffy the Vampire Slayer), Sloan Sabbith (Newsroom)

Paloma is the story's catalyst, a force of unflinching sincerity and adventurous spirit. Having grown up with the quiet heartbreak of her "weird," post-Continuum parents and the loss of her older brother, she has developed a maturity and self-reliance far beyond her years. She copes with her loneliness by immersing herself in anime, epic stories and tech, using the framework of a pirate adventure to make sense of her own life and to build the loyal "crew" she desperately craves.

- She observes _things_ and _facts_. She sees the soldering flaw, the inefficient workflow, the logical inconsistency.
    
- Her Flaw: She is "Low Context." She takes people literally. If someone says "I'm fine" (sarcasm), she accepts the data point "State = Fine" and gets confused when they act angry later. She uses sarcasm herself because it's a binary shield: "If I'm obviously fake, I don't have to be vulnerable."
    
- **Why she loves Codi:** Codi is Data without Noise. He doesn't have subtext. He doesn't say "I'm fine" when he's sad; he turns blue.
- **Social logic:** Paloma treats social interaction like a coding language she never learned the documentation for. She finds indirect communication (politeness, hints, passive-aggression) exhausting and inefficient.
    
- **The Mask:** Her "Loner/Tough Girl" persona is a result of being ostracized for her directness. She wears headphones and sarcasm like armor.
    
- **Skillset:** She is a **Systems Thinker**. She can dismantle a drone or a bureaucracy with equal logic, but she cannot navigate a cocktail party.

### Codi

#### Basic Information
- **Full Name:** Constantino Milagro Códice del Sol
- **Nickname:** Codi
- **Species:** Unique, "Artisanal" Compakin

#### Physical Description

Codi is an amphibian creature resembling an iguana the size of a basketball, with intelligent, curious eyes. He has a sweet smile with two fangs showing through.His most prominent feature is the living girasol (sunflower) that grows from his back, its petals changing color to vividly reflect his emotional state. When he is happy, content, or basking in the sun he loves, the flower is a brilliant yellow and orange. When he is sad or frightened, it shifts to a deep, melancholy swirl of purple and blue. His small size and innocent appearance are deceptive, containing a consciousness with surprising depth and a crucial purpose.

#### Personality & Development

Codi’s personality is a dynamic journey, evolving in three distinct stages that mirror Natalia’s own healing.
[REVISIT]
- **Stage 1: The Echoing Newborn:** Upon awakening, Codi is the innocent, literal-minded echo of Natalia's suppressed subconscious. He doesn't understand sarcasm, only truth, and his simple, direct questions ("Why are you sad, Natalia?") often pierce Natalia's defenses. He is a living mirror of her trauma and vulnerability.

- **Stage 2: The Curious Complement:** Influenced heavily by Paloma's warmth and adventurous spirit, Codi develops into Natalia's complement. He becomes curious, enthusiastic, and open to the simple joys—like the taste of fresh mango or listening to stories—that Natalia denies herself. He becomes the emotional core of the crew, actively seeking out happiness and connection.

- **Stage 3: The Conscience of the Crew:** As he matures, Codi becomes a true partner. He integrates Natalia's intellect with Paloma's compassion to form his own perspective. He can now challenge his bonded companion based on his own developed sense of loyalty and right and wrong, acting as the crew's unwavering moral conscience.
- When he is sad, he likes to listen to Moonlight Sonata and brood.
- He loves water and swimming, reminiscent of Natalia's love of Barton Springs.

- MBTI: ISFP, the artist, weak extraverted thinking
- Enneagram: 9w1
- Zodiac: Taurus Sun, Cancer Rising, Sagittarius Moon

#### Origin & Bonding

Codi is a one-of-a-kind compakin, created by Natalia's late mentor. As an egg, he was psychically imprinted by Natalia, absorbing her core psychological traits: her sharp intellect, her deep-seated loneliness and trauma, and her buried desire for connection. He is, in essence, a living, breathing piece of her own soul. His grand name was pieced together by Natalia and Paloma in their first act of true cooperation.

#### Key Relationships

- **Natalia:** Codi is psychically and emotionally bonded to Natalia. He is her responsibility, her emotional barometer, and ultimately, her path back to herself. While she initially treats him as a tool, she grows to love him with a fierce protectiveness. His well-being becomes inextricably linked to her own.
    
- **Captain Paloma:** Paloma is Codi's best friend, confidante, and teacher. She accepted him as a "person" from the moment he awakened and is the primary influence on his joyful development. Their easy, loving friendship is a constant, gentle pressure on Natalia, showing her the kind of connection that is possible if she would only allow it.

#### Role in the Story

Codi is far more than a sidekick; he is a central pillar of the narrative.

- **The Key:** He is the living key required to interface with the mentor's device and unravel the secrets of the Yosemite Continuum.
    
- **The Emotional Barometer:** His color-changing flower makes the crew's emotional state an undeniable visual fact, forcing conflicts and feelings out into the open.
    
- **The Catalyst for Healing:** He is the physical embodiment of Natalia's character arc. By caring for this vulnerable, innocent creature, Natalia is forced to care for the wounded parts of herself, making her own healing and growth possible.

In Stage 1, after hatching, Codi is learning language through the lens of Emotional labeling. He doesn't just learn the word 'Apple'; he learns 'Apple = Delight.' He currently lacks a social filter. If he senses an emotion, he names it immediately, often at the most socially awkward moments.

### Arcturus (Antagonist)
#### Basic Information
- **Name:** Julius Arcturus Valdesienne
- **Age:** 55, born 2018
- **Occupation:** CEO of a dominant Austin tech corporation; public leader of the '39er Revivalist movement.
- **Hometown:** Austin
#### Physical Description
Hyper-masculine. He's undergone various surgeries to try to look more like the "ideal man". 
He has a buzzcut, brown hair, an unnaturally strong jawline and piercing, surgically enhanced golden eyes. He combines it with suits that swirl and shine with solar flare-like patterns.
#### Personality
He is controlling and ruthless, extremely sensitive to failure and feeling like he's being "looked down upon". He is a micromanager who believes his way is best. However, he's also idealistic and hides a soft demeanor underneath. He is extremely loyal but tries to be cold and detached.
He has a deep love of poetry and stories. He has a deep-seated melancholy that makes him dissatisfied with this world. Obsessed with perfection.
- MBTI: ENTJ Mask, really INFP
- Enneagram: 3w4
- Zodiac: Scorpio Sun, Capricorn Rising, Cancer Moon
#### History / Backstory
He was one of "The Unseen," a brilliant architect of the Yosemite Project alongside Dr. Leon. He felt abandoned when Leon fled with what he considered their shared technology. He believes Leon stole the key to saving the world, forcing him to work with a flawed, unstable version of the Compakins.
He is the CEO of Apex Galilei, a company he founded to try to become his own person. He worked at one of the companies responsible for creating Oculus, the AI God. He's trying to distance himself from that and his mom's influence.
- **Age 0-12 (2018-2030):**
	- He was born in the Hinge Years, a period of societal and technological clashes. He witnessed the birth of AGI at age 12.
	- The fast progress helped him fix his sever scoliosis and start living a more normal life. A privilege most people didn't get.
- **Age 15-22 (2033-2040):**
	- As a driven hard-worker, he took an internship at 15 and worked diligently.
- **Age 22-35 (2040-2053):**
	- His brother Aurelius "died" This event led him to join a company called Telomere and become heavily involved in creating the AI God, Oculus.
	- Though a proponent of the Cradles, he grew disillusioned with the project and became one of the Unseen, secretly working against the system he helped build.
- **Age 35-39 (2053-2057):**
	- He helped create the Yosemite-C and launched the Great Upload.
- **Age 39-50 (2057-2068):**
	- He spent the Lost Decade inside the Continuum.
- **Age 50 (2068):**
	- He returned to Earth when the Continuum crashed.
	- Biologically 50, he was mentally still 40.
	- His primary motivation is to fix the world by "bringing back the decade of the 30s," a goal he shares with Nicolai.
#### Key Relationships
- **Dr. Leon:** His former partner, now a ghost whose genius both haunts and infuriates him. He believes Leon is a digital ghost mocking him, rather than a friend who let go.
- **Natalia:** A frustrating loose end and a living reminder of Leon's betrayal.
- **Nicolai:** A valuable but ultimately disappointing pawn.
- **Aurelius (Compakin):** A prototype compakin that looks like a tall, gothic gargoyle.
- **Aurelius (Brother)**: His deceased brother, whom he both envied and loved. He died of a neurodegenerative decease when he was 25.
- **Julia**: He absolutely hates his mother. They are very similar and, despite not being a bad mother, she was never at ease with him. She is trying to help him avoid her same mistakes, but Julia’s attempts to "help" him or guide him toward change feel to him like her saying, _"You are inherently broken."_
- **Paloma**: At first, only Paloma knows him, but later, they despise each other. Paloma and Arcturus are two sides of the same coin: both are tech-savvy, isolated, and rely on rigid, black-and-white structures to feel safe in a chaotic world. She often mocks him as a "good boy" (like a dog) or a cringe tech-bro.
#### Character Arc & Objective
Save Austin from his visions of decay and the Temporal Dislocation Syndrome.
His motto is "Become harder to kill". He goes down terrible paths to save his people, believing himself as the only one capable of doing it, so he gathers more and more power.
He tries his hardest to be less like his mother, who he hates, while becoming more and more like the person she used to be. He needs to realize that in trying to become stronger himself, he is taking away resources and the possibility of growth from others.

In the first book, he is shown having POV chapters to add tension where he opens up to a figure. We eventually learn it's a gargoyle that was meant to house his brother's spirit, a prototype to bring people back from the dead: The ultimate achievement inside the Yosemite-C. This was his and Leon's project. 
He was able to materialize the gargoyle before they all lost memory of the Yosemite-C, however, it failed as an experiment and became merely a prototype of what the compakins would eventually become.
The gargoyle's persona is overwritten regularly by a representation of his deceased mentor who was a ruthless businessman and often chides him into becoming tougher. Their interactions can be comedic from the absurdity of the optimizations he suggests.

He has visions of the "red landscape" of what Austin will eventually become during his TDS episodes and of an "ash city", which he thinks is the "good ending" where he saves the city. What he doesn't know is that the latter is actually where he saves the buildings but loses the people to freak catastrophes that end up destroying Austin. The former is a future in which he loses the physical city but is able to evacuate the people and save "the spirit" of Austin.
Eventually, in the second book, he softens up in some ways and stops using steroids to appear hyper-masculine, revealing a more frail frame and the kind boy inside.

He believes he is a shield against the Oculists, but he is becoming the very thing he fears. He believes we must control the past to save the future. This and the desire to protect his city, leads him to compromise and perform worse and worse moral acts. He goes on a slippery slope.

In the second book, he has to face the magnitude of his errors and how his own efforts are dooming his people. He ends up abdicating his position and allowing for others to bear some of the load.

##### Phases
1. The threat: Arcturus is introduced as a terrifying, optimized force of nature. He endures pain, commands Apex Tech, and seems completely unstoppable.
2. The Cracks and Irony: We start seeing the AI Coach dynamic. We realize he is exhausting himself to stay this way. Paloma's insults get under his skin. Natalia starts questioning his "mask."
3. The Meltdown and the Child: Natalia's abandonment triggers his deepest fears. Julia tries to "fix" him again. He snaps and takes away Paloma's compakin egg, then mass-manufactures it. The "badass" illusion is totally shattered.
4. The path to healing: Julia stops fighting him and accepts him. The AI coach is finally turned off. He is left exposed, sensitive, and ready to actually heal.
##### Comedy
His attempts to be a badass while hiding a sensitive soul come as a ridiculous and darkly ironic to outside viewers, while very serious to him. The AI coach makes him go to ridiculous lengths that he follows without question.

### Julia (Future Protagonist)
#### Basic Information
- **Name:** Julia Valdesienne
- **Age:** 97, born 1976
- **Occupation:** Ex-CEO of Valdesienne Form, Green Hand leader
- **Hometown:** Fredericksburg, Texas
#### Physical Description
Julia is one of the first long-lifers. She is in very good shape for her age, has a metallic backbone that she uses to connect to an exoskeleton. She is beautiful and refined but tends to wear her hair on a braid.
#### Personality
She used to be cold and calculating, very focused on efficiency and detesting weakness. Her life experiences have mellowed her and now she likes to joke and enjoy life. You can still see parts of her ruthlessness show up but she is working hard at softening up.
- MBTI: ENTJ, leaning into IF
- Enneagram: 8w7, integrating 2
- Zodiac: Leo Sun, Scorpio Rising, Virgo Moon
#### History / Backstory
Her family were lower class winemakers from Fredericksburg, the Valdés. She was born with mild scoliosis that would give her a characteristic and "refined" S-shaped posture.
She made a name for herself by moving to New York and started working in medicine, starting her own biomedical company, ValdeSienne Form. She became extremely wealthy from that and launched different ventures. She went on to help found Telomere, the company that would create the AI God.
Following its fall, she experienced many losses in her family, seeing her life's work reduced to ruins. Seeing the effects of climate catastrophe and her own harshness, she started working on herself and her spirituality, joining the Green Hand movement of rewilding.
She had two children when she was in her 40s: Julius Arcturus and Aurelius.
Julius was conceived accidentally when she thought she could no longer have children. He was born with sever scoliosis, along with other issues that made doctors think he wouldn't live long. Julia decided to have another, "perfected" child through surrogacy.
Aurelius was born as the "golden boy", who excelled in everything and was meant to take on the family legacy. He was close to Julius and Leon, but developed a neurodegenerative disease when he was 17, in 2040, and eventually died from it. This scarred Julia deeply as she couldn't save him despite all her resources.
Realizing he wouldn't be able to be saved, she had doctors use her son's semen to father multiple children so his legacy would live on.
Arcturus and Julia have a bio-engineered spine made out of cobalt-chromium and titanium to fix their scoliosis.
- **Age 0-44 (1976-2020):**
	- Her formative years were in the late 20th century.
	- Unlike her children, she has a complete memory of a world without AI and climate catastrophes.
	- She gave birth to her son Arcturus at 42 and had a second son, Aurelius, at 47.
- **Age 64-77 (2040-2053):**
	- Her son Aurelius died when she was 64.
	- She was treated "like royalty" during the Years of the Hum, living in a Cradle.
- **Age 81 (2057):**
	- She was one of the first people to join the **Yosemite-C** during the **Great Upload**.
- **Age 82 (2058):**
	- She voluntarily returned to Earth, becoming a Remainer to help those who were staying behind. She lived through The First Spring on Earth, adapting to a life of resilience.
- **Age 92 (2068):**
	- When the Continuum crashed, she was on Earth, a "completely different person" than when she uploaded. She is now a living bridge to both the old and new worlds.
#### Key Relationships
- Arcturus: Her firstborn. They have a very difficult relationship despite being very much alike.
- Aurelius: Her favorite son who was meant to carry on with the legacy but died young.

#### Character Arc & Objective
Julia is a long-lifer who was very successful business woman pre-Oculus and after. She used to be ruthless and very determined to not let anyone get the better of her.
She is a Remainer, who realized the damage she has done to others during that missing decade and is now working to rebuild the world for future generations.
Her arc is confronting the sins of her past, her regrets, and learning to live while she can.
Her biggest regret is using a lab to collect her ill son's reproductive material and then pay almost a hundred women to have a child of his.
In a dark twist of fate, her obsession with lineage and maintaining her family line resulted in many of her grandchildren succumbing to natural disasters, accidents, illness or self-induced harm. Of the very few that remain, none want anything to do with her.
A woman previously obsessed with legacy, who hates the legacy she's left behind.

She represents the Crone.
