## The Unseen

### Kaelo Al-Shabani
#### Basic Information
- **Full Name:** Kaelo Al-Shabani 
- **Age:** 61, born 2012
- **Occupation (Current):** Pan-African Revivalist Leader
- **Occupation (Former):** Leader of the Unseen. Meta Architect for Oculus.
- **Hometown:** Lamu, Kenya. Studied in Lagos, Nigeria and in the Netherlands.
#### Physical Description
Slender African man who is blind and deaf, but uses haptic sensors to perceive the world. He walks with a cane and welcomes the effects of aging, while also challenging some of them.
#### Personality
Resilient and hopeful. He is extremely curious.
#### History / Backstory
- Born in Lamu to Fatima Al-Shabani, influential matriarch, and Dr. Adebayo Okoro, a Nigerian-French ethnomusicologist who was studying Taarab music in Lamu when he fell in love with Fatima.
- When he was in his teens, he lost a young brother and got brain damage due to a freak storm.
- He started to lose his sight and hearing due to brain damage. His father helped him with research and studies, while his mother connected his other senses to Lamu. He started a project to create haptic sensory.
- In his late teens, he lost his mother to another storm while she was protecting people of Lamu who couldn't escape. Lamu was permanently damaged and he was forced to escape to Lagos with his father, who was also changed and became a husk of himself.
- Kaelo studied Software Engineering in Lagos and improved his haptic sensors.
- Climate refugee crises forced Kaelo to have to leave for the Netherlands with an old peer of his dad. Meanwhile, his dad got lost in the chaos and Kaelo lost touch with him.
- In the Netherlands, he experienced a toxic dynamic with Dr. Annelies de Vries who stole his project and gave it over to the AI God, Oculus. He developed the virtual reality that would be used in the Cradles later on.
- He pretended to be aligned with Oculus and worked in the shadows, finding the flaws of the system and becoming the first of the Unseen. Recruiting others.
- He spoke to Natalia when she was a teen and inspired her, along with other youth, to escape the bindings of Oculus, leading the resistance against the AI God.
- He collaborated with Leon closely, later pushing the Revivalist movement in Africa and making Nigeria a leading hub for restoration.
#### Key Relationships
- Dr Leon: Close allies.
- Julia: He distrusts her but is finding potential for an alliance.
- Natalia: Her inspiration. He remembers her faintly.
#### Character Arc & Objective / Role
- He is the legend and a veteran. He knows the world is not as it seems and navigates it carefully. He doesn't like to get involved directly, but rather to support the causes that matter.
