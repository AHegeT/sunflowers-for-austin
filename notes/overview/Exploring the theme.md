Let's come up with a question, 4 perspectives, the plot and choose the protagonist from there.
Rather than starting with the protagonist.

Questions
- What does a hopeful future look like?

- How do we heal after heartbreak?

- How do we cope with loss?

- How do build back after utter failure?

Drives
- Guilt and forgiveness
- Community vs Individualism

Question
In the wake of utter failure, is true healing found in restoring what was lost, or in embracing what  we have become to build something new?

- True healing is found in restoring the past
	- The Restorationist
		- Breaking point: Fails to restore the past, so he shifts into thinking we are monsters beyond forgiveness
		- Evolution: Accepts the monster and believes this is his ultimate form
	- Arcturus
		- Tries to go back to the "golden age"
		- Believes in the motto of becoming harder to kill
		- Radical humanist but also leans individualist and Darwinist
- True healing is building something new that rejects the past
	- The Rejectionist
		- Breaking point: Fails to keep the past at bay, it takes him over
		- Evolution: Finds there were people in his past who love him and accept what he's done
	- Monty
		- Wants to build to move away from the past
		- Calm, kind and collected, but only by creating a safe haven from the old world
		- He used to be a violent rebel, eventually that trauma gets the best of him
- True healing is understanding the failure and daring to integrate it with our original vision
	- The Integrationist
		- Breaking point: By trying to integrate everything, even the monster, ends up standing for nothing
		- Evolution: Chooses between perspectives and fights with conviction for what she believes in
	- Natalia
		- A therapist who wants to help others but struggles to understand herself
		- Will eventually betray her friend by trying to understand the antagonist and losing herself in the process
- We cannot find true healing, but we can build it for others
	- The Penitent
		- Breaking point: By not allowing herself to heal, she ends up infecting others 
		- Evolution: She finally allows herself to be a part of the future she built, transitioning from a detached martyr to a participant.
	- Julia
		- Arcturus's mother, although their bond is severed
		- A rich woman who underwent life extension procedures and led everyone to this mess
		- She was obsessed with her legacy and ended up destroying it by trying to preserve it
		- Now, she seeks atonement through self-sacrifice.
- True healing is embracing the monster we have become
	- The Nihilist
		- Breaking point: In his quest to prove nothing is worth saving, he almost destroys the one thing he secretly values
		- Evolution: They find meaning in the monster and try to guide others in their last moments
	- Leon
		- Natalia's old mentor and Arcturus's old best friend
		- He believes the failure was inevitable and that the only honest path is to lean into the darkness.
		- Orchestrates the inciting incidents and presumably kills himself
- True healing doesn't matter, only being able to function
	- The Pragmatist
		- Breaking point: By focusing on the future, the wound stays open and it festers
		- Evolution: Integrating other people's perspectives, becoming a leader
	- Paloma
		- Teenager who was born after the worst of the disasters
		- Really good with tech and not very concerned about the past
		- Natalia's companion
- There is no true healing, we can only heal ourselves
	- The Individualist
		- Breaking point: His selfishness results in him abandoning the people he actually cares about, leaving him truly alone in a world that requires community.
		- Evolution: He finally puts the needs of others ahead of his own, discovering a deeper sense of self through protection.
	- Nicolai
		- Natalia's childhood friend and Arcturus's right hand man
		- Seemingly very put-together and charming, but also very shallow and naive about the world
- There is no true healing and I do not need it, this is my ultimate form
- We cannot find true healing, we are monsters beyond forgiveness

# Dynamics

## Teachers

- Natalia and Monty
	- Mutual Healing
	- Can the truth set you free, or just break you?
- Paloma and Julia
	- Pragmatic Atonement
	- The fixer and the cause for everything to be broken
	- The legacy and future generation she was so desperate for

## High Friction
- Arcturus and Monty
	- Ideological War
	- The Gilded Age or the Great Decay?
	- Burying the past vs digging it out
	- Is the past a map or a cage?
- Natalia and Leon
	- The bridge vs the fire

## Mirrors
- Natalia and Nicolai
	- The Split Path
- Leon and Monty
	- The unrepentant and the one trying to keep the darkness at bay
	- Leon is what Monty is trying to keep at bay
- Monty and Nicolai
	- The perfect ally vs the peaceful builder
	- Mirrors of insecurity
	- The resolution: The shield and the sword

Peers
- Leon and Julia
	- Leon is the only one who truly knows and understands the gravity of what Julia has done
	- She sees him as beyond salvation, another "child" she's pushed past the brink of sanity
	- There is a mutual respect, however
	- Julia understands why Leon wants to burn everything down
- Monty and Paloma
	- She wants to scavenge the ruins, he wants to bury them
	- Monty wants to protect Paloma from the violence of the past
- Natalia and Paloma
	- The over-thinker and the doer
	- Natalia tries to understand too much so Paloma finds it exhausting, but learns from it
	- Paloma helps Natalia stay grounded and focus on action so she doesn't lose herself in others
- Paloma and Nicolai
	- A comedic pairing
	- Paloma sees past his charm and makes fun of him
	- He sees Paloma as simple-minded at first
	- Eventually, Nicolai grows to care about Paloma

Potential answer
True healing doesn't lie in restoring what was lost or in embracing the monster we've become as an end-state, but rather in nurturing the monster into something better.
