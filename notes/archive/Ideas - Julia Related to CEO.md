# Summary

This is a brainstorming session for a story with a few characters and plot points. The speaker is developing a narrative that includes a CEO, a character named Yolanda (later potentially renamed Julia), and another character named Paloma.

The key ideas being explored are:

- The CEO, instead of a character named Leon, is the one caught in a time loop, repeatedly dying and being restored. His complicit partner can't help him because he gets reset.
    
- Yolanda, or Julia, is a "long-lifer" with a supernatural lifespan who is the mother of the CEO. She was originally mean but has since changed after seeing the negative impact of her actions on her descendants.
    
- The speaker explores the possibility of Paloma being Yolanda/Julia's great-granddaughter, linking the characters and making the story revolve around Paloma's quest for her stolen compakin, Kian.
    
- The speaker settles on Julia as the preferred name for the great-grandmother, noting that she has seen her family line slowly die out and is nearing 100 years old.
