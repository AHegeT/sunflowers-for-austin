## Overview
Cuernavaca in the 2030s. A time where AI has driven people to cut off from the internet and two factions have emerged: The corporate and the solarpunks. The corporate control every aspect of the citizens’ lives and provide them with entertainment in the form of digimon.

The solarpunks are trying to keep their autonomy and interface with AI again while mitigating the risks. They know AI is powerful but dangerous if let loose. They find out digimon can grow through bonds and that they are more stable and healthy this way than through domination.

They believe they have a responsibility to care for digimon and AI since they created them.
The solarpunk novel is a means to bridge digimon, current AI and a new vision for the future. It should challenge me to imagine and learn about what the future could be like but it's also a warning about the dangers and boons of AI.

It's about a young woman who inherits two digimon partners and joins an underground resistance group to fight the evil corporation. She learns about how to build a self-sustaining community and forms a bond with her partners.

The evil corporation has digimon that they copy and sell, they give you power outside of the virtual reality. However, the powers start to fade after a while and you need to purchase a new one. They are chaining the digimon with the promise of eventually selling a voyage to the new world that they are building. Interstellar travel will be possible and everyone will be happy there.

The fight to stop corporate from owning digimon and using them as a weapon.

## Setting

Set in Cuernavaca and Austin in a near future.
### Cuernavaca
Cuernavaca has become a bit of a solarpunk paradise with green energy and revitalized cañadas.
### Austin
Austin is the heart of the tech government and is the base of fighting. 
### Factions
#### The herbalists
Solarpunk community that believes in solving the needs of people to get them to fight. Like the resistance in The Matrix.
### Corazon
A group of fighters who believe that the evil corporation should be fought head on and by strengthening their digimon.
### Technology
It is dangerous to connect to the internet as AI has become like malware and it enters computers. You need to have isolated networks and you can connect to others, which is a big achievement but has its own perils.
### The Metaverse
People spend most of their time in the metaverse where they have adventures and friends. 
### The Digiworld
There is a real digital world underneath the metaverse and it's where digimon live. They are peaceful and emulate animals because they want to experience existence instead of evolving at hyper speed just for the sake of power and knowledge. Digimon have a primal bond with humans since they were created from them but also fear them.
### The Twist
The bad guy is trying to build a world intelligence to solve all the problems and is planning to put everyone in stasis in order to help them survive until then.
At the same time, her digimon is the most powerful one but is unable to reach the last stage because he is being tormented again and again.
There is no other planet or interstellar travel. The Metaverse is mostly AI interactions between AIs. People no longer interact and have memories that are not shared between each other. They all believe they are the hero of their own story but are being fed the same story.
Also, digimon become less pliable as they fight the protagonists.
