The Scraphorn: "Bebop"
Paloma has a tool she made from scrap that plays brief sounds, she calls it her acoustic disruptor or **Bebop**. It isn't just a tool; it's her social interface. She treats it like a pet, often "petting" the haptic sensors to calm herself down.

- **Mode 1: The Womp-Womp (The Shield):** The classic "Sad Trombone" used to deflect bullying by mocking the bully first.
    
- **Mode 2: The Fanfare (The Spark):** A triumphant, over-the-top anime-style heraldry she plays to "hype" herself up before soldering or when a logic gate finally clicks.
    
- **Mode 3: The Rimshot/Stinger (The Emphasis):** Used for perfect comedic timing, which usually confuses her "Low Context" peers but makes perfect sense to her.
