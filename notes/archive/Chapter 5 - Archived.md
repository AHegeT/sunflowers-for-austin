Natalia had been around many tier II patients, whose disconnection from reality was more emotional than logical. Paloma's parents clearly could take care of themselves for the most part. But they did have a nurse watching over them. She felt such terrible sadness.

The worst part is she wasn't even sure if she could treat them. The best way to treat TDS was to prevent it from getting worse. She knew accounts of patients improving their treatment from tier II to I. But the accounts became hearsay from tier III to II, and even rarer from tier IV. However, she knew many cases of the wrong treatment leading a tier III patient into a IV. Particularly around attempts to "snap them back" to reality.

  

The parents were polite and seemed even peaceful. But when interacting with them, there was this coldness, like they were talking through a plastic sphere.

Could Leon have been trying to prevent more of these cases?

The comment was too accurate to ignore. Paloma wasn't upset because Natalia was putting herself in danger, she was seeing her do the same thing to Codi that she had probably endured with her parents when they came back. She made a mental note to end the experiments for now.
